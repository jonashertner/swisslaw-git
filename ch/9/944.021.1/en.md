# EAER Ordinance on the Declaration for Timber and Timber Products

English is not an official language of the Swiss Confederation. This translation is provided for information purposes only and has no legal force.

of 7 June 2010 (Status as of 1 January 2017)

The Federal Department of Economic Affairs Education and Research (EAER[^ingress-1]),

based on Articles 1 paragraph 2 and 2 paragraph 2 the Ordinance of 4 June 2010[^ingress-2] on the Declaration for Timber and Timber Products,

ordains:

[^ingress-1]: The name of this administrative unit was amended in application of Art. 16 para. 3 of the Publications Ordinance of 17 Nov. 2004 ([AS 2004 4937](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2004/746)) on 1 Jan. 2013. The amendment has been made throughout the text.
[^ingress-2]: [SR 944.021](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/2010/379)

#### Art. 1

**Timber and timber products subject to declaration**

The duty to declare applies to timber and timber products specified in the Annex.

#### Art. 2

**Reference system for the declaration of timber species**

The reference system for the declaration of the timber species comprises:

- a. the commercial timber list of the Swiss Timber Trade Centre[^2-1]; and
- b. Standard SN EN 13556:2003[^2-2].

[^2-1]: The list may be inspected under: www.konsum.admin.ch
[^2-2]: The standards may be inspected free of charge and obtained for a fee from the Swiss Association for Standardization (SNV), Sulzerallee 70, 8404 Winterthur; www.snv.ch

#### Art. 3

**Commencement**

This Ordinance comes into force on 1 October 2010.
