## Section 1 General Provisions

#### Art. 1

**Subject matter**

This Ordinance governs the fees and supervisory levies for precious metals control, namely:[^1-1]

- a. fees for the services and decisions of the Central Office for Precious Metals Control, the federal assay offices and the cantonal assay offices;
- b. fees for assays of fineness carried out by trade assayers in accordance with Article 41 of the Precious Metals Control Act;
- c. fees for services provided by the Central Office;
- d.[^1-2] supervisory levies for costs that are not covered by the fees for supervision of commercial purchases of melt material in accordance with Article 36 paragraph 2 letter e PMCA or supervision in accordance with Article 42<sup>ter</sup> PMCA.

[^1-1]: Amended by Annex No 5 of the O of 31 Aug. 2022, in force since 1 Jan. 2023 ([AS 2022 552](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2022/552)).
[^1-2]: Inserted by Annex No 5 of the O of 31 Aug. 2022, in force since 1 Jan. 2023 ([AS 2022 552](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2022/552)).

#### Art. 2

**Liability to pay fees**[^2-1]

<sup>1</sup>

A fee must be paid by any person who:

- a. requests a decision in accordance with Article 1 letter a;
- b. uses a service in accordance with Article 1 letter a;
- c. requests supervisory proceedings which do not culminate in a decision or which are abandoned.

<sup>2</sup> Federal, cantonal and communal authorities are not required to pay for precious metals control services in the context of legal and administrative assistance.

[^2-1]: Amended by Annex No 5 of the O of 31 Aug. 2022, in force since 1 Jan. 2023 ([AS 2022 552](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2022/552)).

#### Art. 3

**Applicability of the General Fees Ordinance**

Unless otherwise stipulated in this Ordinance, the provisions of the General Fees Ordinance of 8 September 2004[^3-1] apply.

[^3-1]: [SR 172.041.1](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/2004/677)

#### Art. 4

**Calculation[^4-1]**

<sup>1</sup> The fees and supervisory levies are calculated in accordance with the rates specified in the Annex.[^4-2]

<sup>2</sup> For services and decisions for which no rate is specified in the Annex, the fee is based on time spent (Art. 14).

<sup>3</sup> Fees and levies are periodically adjusted to take account of the services actually performed, the costs incurred and technological developments.[^4-3]

## Section 2 Official Analysis and Hallmarking

#### Art. 5

**Principles**

The fees for the official Swiss hallmarking of watch-cases and other precious metal articles and mixed metal articles comprise:

- a. the fee for conformity testing;
- b. the hallmarking fee.

#### Art. 6

**Fee for conformity testing**

<sup>1</sup> For conformity testing of watch-cases and other precious metal articles and mixed metal articles, the fees specified in the Annex apply.

<sup>2</sup> For objects comprised of a combination of several precious metals, the rates for each individual metal are added together.

<sup>3</sup> Certified material means precious metals and precious metal alloys whose fineness before production is confirmed with a conformity certificate that is issued or recognised by one of the bodies under Article 1 letter a.

#### Art. 7

**Hallmarking fees**

<sup>1</sup> For applying the hallmark on precious metal and mixed metal watch-cases or other articles, the fees specified in the Annex apply.

<sup>2</sup>

A single mark is:

- a. the official Swiss hallmark; or
- b. the international hallmark in accordance with the Convention of 15 November 1972[^7-1] on the Control and Marking of Articles of Precious Metals.

<sup>3</sup> A double mark is the combined Swiss/international hallmark. The double mark is applied as a single unit. The fee for a double mark is charged even if individual marks are applied separately and by the manufacturer itself.

[^7-1]: [SR 0.941.31](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/1975/1013_1014_1013)

#### Art. 8

**Acceptance fee**

<sup>1</sup> If a series of fewer than ten objects is submitted for official hallmarking, an acceptance fee as specified in the Annex will be charged.

<sup>2</sup> The acceptance fee is not charged if the official hallmarks are applied by the applicant itself.

#### Art 9

**Official hallmarking agreements**

<sup>1</sup>

For the conclusion of an agreement in accordance with Article 97 paragraph 2 and Article 117*a* paragraph 3 of the Ordinance on the Control of the Trade in Precious Metals and Precious Metal Articles of 8 May 1934[^9-1] (PMCO), the fees specified in the Annex apply:

- a. for creating the dossier and reviewing the application;
- b. for the audits carried out during the term of the agreement in accordance with Article 97 PMCO or for its renewal;
- c. for the additional audit in the case of a request for an agreement in accordance with Article 117*a* PMCO.

<sup>2</sup> The fees specified in paragraph 1 are payable before the conclusion or renewal of the agreement.

<sup>3</sup> The fee for onsite supervision based on Article 117*a* paragraph 2 PMCO is based on time spent (Art. 14).

<sup>4</sup> The Central Office charges an annual fee for the accreditation of suppliers and testing laboratories for certified material in accordance with the Annex.

[^9-1]: [SR 941.311](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/50/363_375_420)

## Section 3 Fees for Fineness Assays

#### Art. 10

**Assay of the fineness of samples**

<sup>1</sup> A sample is defined as an object, a piece or any type of specimen taken from the same material which does not correspond to a parcel or a melted product.

<sup>2</sup> For assaying the fineness of samples, the fees specified in the Annex apply.

<sup>3</sup> The fee covers the number of analyses necessary to assay the fineness of the sample.

<sup>4</sup> Reduced-scope fineness assays include analyses for conformity with the legal standard of fineness on a single analysed metal.

<sup>5</sup> The fee for assaying the fineness is doubled in the case of arbitration analysis.

<sup>6</sup> For assays of fineness that cannot be carried out using standardised testing methods, the fees are based on time spent.

#### Art. 11

**Assay of the fineness of melted products**

<sup>1</sup> For assaying the fineness of melted products, the fees specified in the Annex apply.

<sup>2</sup> For the assay and marking of each melted product, an additional fee is charged as specified in the Annex.

#### Art. 12

**Fees for conversion into analysable form**

For the conversion into analysable form of material, solutions, salts and other materials which, due to their composition, require prior chemical and physical digestion, further fees as specified in the Annex are charged in addition to the fees set out in Articles 10 and 11.

## Section 4 Other Flat-Rate Fees

#### Art. 13

<sup>1</sup>

Other flat-rate fees apply to:

- a. licences;
- b. registrations of responsibility marks;
- c. awarding of diplomas;
- d.[^13-1] ongoing supervision of trade assayers, melters, commercial purchasers of melt material and the cantonal assay offices;
- e. courses for precious metal assayers from industry or the cantonal assay offices;
- f. conformity testing of new coatings and materials.

<sup>2</sup> The fees are calculated in accordance with the rates specified in the Annex.

[^13-1]: Amended by Annex No 5 of the O of 31 Aug. 2022, in force since 1 Jan. 2023 ([AS 2022 552](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2022/552)).

## Section 5 Hourly Fees

#### Art. 14

**Principle[^14-1]**

<sup>1</sup> For services, in particular expert opinions and decisions, for which no rate is specified in the Annex, a fee of CHF 90 to 135 per hour is charged.

<sup>2</sup> The exact fee within the range given in paragraph 1 is determined on the basis of the expertise required.

<sup>3</sup> Hours may be divided into quarters of an hour. Each fraction of a quarter of an hour is counted as one full quarter of an hour.

[^14-1]: Inserted by Annex No 5 of the O of 31 Aug. 2022, in force since 1 Jan. 2023 ([AS 2022 552](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2022/552)).

#### Art. 14a

**Fees relating to the supervision of trading in banking precious metals**[^14a-1]

<sup>1</sup> The fees for audits, supervisory proceedings and decisions relating to trading in banking precious metals (Art. 42<sup>ter</sup> PMCA) are based on time spent.

<sup>2</sup> The hourly rate for the fees specified in paragraph 1 is CHF 250–350, depending on the specialist knowledge and functional level of the person carrying out the task at the Central Office.

[^14a-1]: Inserted by Annex No 5 of the O of 31 Aug. 2022, in force since 1 Jan. 2023 ([AS 2022 552](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2022/552)).

## Section 5*a*: Supervisory Levies

[^sec5a-1]

[^sec5a-1]: Inserted by Annex No 5 of the O of 31 Aug. 2022, in force since 1 Jan. 2023 ([AS 2022 552](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2022/552)).

#### Art. 14b

**Principle, scope and calculation basis**

<sup>1</sup> Purchasers of melt material in accordance with Article 31*a* PMCA, trade assayers and group companies in accordance with Article 42<sup>bis</sup> PMCA must pay an annual supervisory levy to the Central Office.

<sup>2</sup> Purchasers of melt material in accordance with Article 31*a* PMCA shall pay the levy in the form of a lump sum for a period of four years.

<sup>3</sup> Trade assayers and group companies in accordance with Article 42<sup>bis</sup> PMCA that hold a licence for commercial trading in banking precious metals from the Central Office shall pay the levy in the form of a fixed basic levy and a variable supplementary levy.

<sup>4</sup> The supplementary levy covers the costs that are not covered by the basic levy.

#### Art. 14c

**Basic levy**

For the basic levy in accordance with Article 14*b* paragraph 3, the rate in the Annex applies.

#### Art. 14d

**Supplementary levy**

As regards the amount to be covered via the supplementary levy in accordance with Article 13*b* paragraph 3, one tenth of such levy shall be based on the balance sheet total and nine tenths on the gross revenue.

#### Art. 14e

**Calculation of the supplementary levy**

<sup>1</sup>

For calculating the supplementary levy based on the balance sheet total and gross revenue, the profit and loss account of the entity subject to the levy in accordance with Article 959*b* of the Code of Obligations[^14e-1], as reported in the approved financial statements for the year preceding the year to which the levy applies, shall be decisive. The following may be deducted for trading in banking precious metals:

- a. if a profit and loss account by nature of expense is used: volume changes in unfinished and finished goods, as well as the cost of purchased materials;
- b. if a profit and loss account by function of expense is used: the costs of procuring or manufacturing the purchased products.

<sup>2</sup> The decisive factor in calculating the supplementary levy based on the balance sheet total and gross revenue shall be the result in the financial statements for the year preceding the year to which the levy applies.

<sup>3</sup> The calculation of the supplementary levy according to gross revenue shall be based exclusively on the gross revenue of the business activity subject to the licence requirement in accordance with Article 42<sup>bis</sup> PMCA.

[^14e-1]: [SR 220](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/27/317_321_377)

#### Art. 14f

**Start and end of levy liability**

<sup>1</sup> Levy liability shall begin with the granting of the licence and end with its withdrawal or the discharge from supervision.

<sup>2</sup> If the start or end of levy liability does not coincide with the start or end of the year to which the levies apply, the basic levy and supplementary levy shall be payable *pro rata temporis*.

<sup>3</sup> A right to reimbursement based on paragraph 2 at the end of levy liability shall exist only for amounts exceeding CHF 1000.

#### Art. 14g

**Collection of levies**

<sup>1</sup> The Central Office shall collect the supervisory levies from the licence holders in accordance with Article 42<sup>bis</sup> PMCA based on its cost accounting for the year preceding the year to which the levies apply.

<sup>2</sup> After finalising its cost accounting, the Central Office shall produce an invoice for each entity subject to the levies.

#### Art. 14h

**Invoicing, due dates, deferral and expiry**

<sup>1</sup> The Central Office shall produce invoices for the supervisory levies.

<sup>2</sup> If the entities subject to the levies do not agree with the final invoice, they may request an appealable decision.

<sup>3</sup> Due dates, deferral and expiry shall be based by analogy on the provisions of the General Fees Ordinance of 8 September 2004[^14h-1].

[^14h-1]: [SR 172.041.1](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/2004/677)

## Section 6 Compensation for Services provided by the Central Office

#### Art. 15

<sup>1</sup> The cantonal assay offices pay an annual fee to the Central Office.

<sup>2</sup> This consists of a fixed sum and a variable amount depending on the gross turnover generated by the fees charged in accordance with Articles 5 to 9.

<sup>3</sup> The compensation is governed by an administrative service level agreement.
