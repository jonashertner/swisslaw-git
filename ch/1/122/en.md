# Federal Act on the Proscription of the Groups «Al-Qaeda» and «Islamic State» and Associated Organisations

English is not an official language of the Swiss Confederation. This translation is provided for information purposes only and has no legal force.

of 12 December 2014 (Status as of 1 January 2019)

The Federal Assembly of the Swiss Confederation,

based on Articles 54 paragraph 1 and 173 paragraph 2 of the Federal Constitution[^ingress-1], and having considered the Federal Council Dispatch of 12 November 2014[^ingress-2],

decrees:

[^ingress-1]: [SR 101](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/1999/404)
[^ingress-2]: [BBl 2014 8925](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/fga/2014/1958)

#### Art. 1

**Proscription**

The following groups and organisations are proscribed:

- a. the group «al-Qaeda»;
- b. the group «Islamic State»;
- c. cover and successor groups to «al-Qaeda» or the «Islamic State» and organisations and groups that correspond in their leadership, aims and methods to «al-Qaeda» or the «Islamic state» or which act on their behalf.

#### Art. 2

**Criminal provisions**

<sup>1</sup> Any person who on Swiss territory participates in a group or organisation proscribed under Article 1, supports such a group in human resources or material terms, organises propaganda campaigns for them or for their aims, recruits for them or promotes their activities in any other way shall be liable to a custodial sentence not exceeding five years or to a monetary penalty.

<sup>2</sup> Any person who commits any of the foregoing acts abroad also commits an offence if he or she is arrested in Switzerland and not extradited. Article 7 paragraphs 4 and 5 of the Swiss Criminal Code[^2-1] apply.

<sup>3</sup> The prosecution and adjudication of the acts described in paragraphs 1 and 2 are subject to federal jurisdiction.

[^2-1]: [SR 311.0](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/54/757_781_799)

#### Art. 3

**Forfeiture of assets**

The general provisions of the Criminal Code[^3-1] on the forfeiture of assets apply, and in particular Articles 70 paragraph 5 and 72.

[^3-1]: [SR 311.0](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/54/757_781_799)

#### Art. 4

**Referendum, commencement and term of validity**

<sup>1</sup> This Act is declared urgent (Art. 165 para. 1 Federal Constitution). It is subject to an optional referendum (Art. 141 para. 1 let. b Federal Constitution).

<sup>2</sup> It comes into force on 1 January 2015 and applies until 31 December 2018.

<sup>3</sup> The term of validity of this Act shall be extended until 31 December 2022.[^4-1]

[^4-1]: Inserted by No I of the FA of 15 June 2018, in force since 1 Jan. 2019 ([AS 2018 3345](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2018/542); [BBl 2018 87](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/fga/2018/48)).
