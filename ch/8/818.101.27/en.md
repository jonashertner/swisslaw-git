# Ordinance on Measures to Combat the Coronavirus (COVID-19) in International Passenger Transport

English is not an official language of the Swiss Confederation. This translation is provided for information purposes only and has no legal force.

(COVID-19 Ordinance on International Passenger Transport Measures)

of 2 July 2020 (Status as of 15 January 2021)

The Swiss Federal Council,

on the basis of Article 41 paragraph 3 of the Epidemics Act of 28 September 2012[^ingress-1] (EpidA),

ordains:

[^ingress-1]: [SR 818.101](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/2015/297)

#### Art. 1

**Subject matter and purpose**

This Ordinance introduces measures in relation to international movement of persons to prevent the cross-border spread of the Sars-Cov-2 coronavirus.

#### Art. 2

**Quarantine for persons entering Switzerland**[^2-1]

<sup>1</sup> Persons who enter Switzerland and who have within the previous 10 days stayed for any time in a country or area with an increased risk of infection with the Sars-Cov-2 coronavirus (country or area with an increased risk of infection) are required immediately on entry to go directly to their home or to other suitable accommodation. They must remain there continuously for 10 days following their entry (quarantine).

<sup>2</sup> If a person enters Switzerland via a country or area that does not have an increased risk of infection, the competent cantonal authority may deduct the time spent in that country or area from the period that must be spent in quarantine in accordance with paragraph 1.

[^2-1]: Amended by No I of the O of 11 Sept. 2020, in force since 14 Sept. 2020 ([AS 2020 3699](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2020/672)).

#### Art. 3

**Country or area with an increased risk of infection**

<sup>1</sup>

There is an increased risk of infection with the Sars-Cov-2 coronavirus if at least one of the following requirements is satisfied:

- a.[^3-1] In the past 14 days the number of new infections per 100 000 persons in the country or area concerned is more than 60 higher than in Switzerland, and this number is not due to specific incidents or cases occurring in a narrowly defined geographical area.
- b. The available information from the country or area concerned does not allow a reliable assessment to be made of the risk situation, and there are indications that there is an increased risk of transmission in the country or area concerned.
- c. In the past four weeks, there have been repeated instances of infected persons who have stayed in the country or area concerned entering Switzerland.
- d.[^3-2] In the country or area concerned, a mutation of the coronavirus Sars-CoV-2 is spreading that carries a higher risk of infection than the form of the virus that is prevalent in Switzerland.

<sup>1bis</sup> Areas on the border with Switzerland with which close economic, social and cultural exchanges take place may be exempted from inclusion on the list mentioned in paragraph 2 even if they meet the requirements set out in paragraph 1.[^3-3]

<sup>2</sup> The list of countries or areas with an increased risk of infection is set out in the Annex. The Federal Department of Home Affairs (FDHA) shall update the list regularly in consultation with the Federal Department of Justice and Police (FDJP), the Federal Department of Finance (FDF) and the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDFA).

[^3-1]: Amended by No I of the O of 28 Oct. 2020 (Redefining States and Areas with an Increased Rate of Infection; Exceptions from the Quarantine Requirement), in force since 29 Oct. 2020 ([AS 2020 4513](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2020/794)).
[^3-2]: Inserted by No I of the O of 21 Dec. 2020 (Inclusion of the United Kingdom and South Africa), in force from 1pm on 21 Dec. 2020 ([AS 2020 6399](https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2020/1047)).

#### Art. 4

**Exceptions to the quarantine requirement**

<sup>1</sup>

The following persons are exempted from the quarantine requirement under Article 2:

- a. persons who transport other persons or goods professionally by road, rail, ship or plane;
- b.

  persons whose work is essential to maintain:

  - 1\. the efficiency of the health service,
  - 2\. public security and order,
  - 3\. the efficiency of institutional beneficiaries as defined in Article 2 paragraph 1 of the Host State Act of 22 June 2007[^4-1],
  - 4\.[^4-2] Switzerland’s diplomatic and consular relations;

- c. persons who in the course of their professional activities for rail, bus, shipping or airline companies transport passengers across borders and in doing so have spent time in a country or area with an increased risk of infection;
- d.[^4-3] persons who enter Switzerland for compelling professional or medical reasons and whose visit cannot be postponed;
- e. persons who as transit passengers have stayed less than 24 hours in a country or area with an increased risk of infection;
- f. persons who enter Switzerland for the purpose of transiting the country and who intend and are able to travel on directly to another country;
- g.[^4-4] persons who re-enter Switzerland after participating in an event in a country or area with an increased risk of infection, provided they prove that their participation and stay took place in compliance with a specific precautionary measures plan; participating in an event includes in particular participating, normally in a professional capacity, in a sports competition or cultural event, or in a professional conference;
- h.[^4-5] persons who re-enter Switzerland after a stay in a country or area with an increased risk of infection for compelling professional or medical reasons which could not be postponed.

<sup>1bis</sup> On entry from countries and areas in accordance with Article 3 paragraph 1 letter d, the exceptions to the quarantine requirements in paragraph 1 letters d and h do not apply.[^4-6]

<sup>2</sup> The employer shall verify and certify that entering Switzerland is essential in terms of paragraph 1 letter b.

<sup>3</sup> The competent cantonal authority may in justified cases permit further exceptions from the quarantine requirement or relax certain conditions.

<sup>4</sup> For persons who display the symptoms of COVID-19, paragraph 1 does not apply unless the symptoms have a different cause.

#### Art. 5

**Duty to notify for persons entering Switzerland**

Any person who is required to go into quarantine in accordance with this Ordinance must notify the competent cantonal authority within two days of his or her arrival in Switzerland and follow the instructions they are given by this authority.

#### Art. 6a

**Transitional provision to the Amendment of 21 December 2020**[^6a-1]

Persons who entered Switzerland from the United Kingdom or South Africa on or after 14 December 2020 must go into quarantine by midnight on 21 December 2020 at the latest. Notification in accordance with Article 5 must be given by noon on 24 December.
